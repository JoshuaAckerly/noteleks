# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Graveyard Jokes Studios ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you play Noteleks (noteleks.graveyardjokes.com).

## 1. Information We Collect

Noteleks is a browser-based game. We collect minimal information:

- **Session data**: A temporary session cookie is created when you visit to enable basic site functionality (CSRF protection). No personal identifying information is stored in the session.
- **Basic server logs**: Standard web server logs may include your IP address and browser type. These logs are used for security monitoring and are not retained long-term.

We do not require user registration, email addresses, or any personal information to play the game.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the minimal information collected to:

- Ensure site security and integrity
- Monitor for abuse or attacks

## 3. Third-Party Services

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

## 4. Cookies

We use only a single essential session cookie required for site security. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) for details.

## 5. Data Retention

Session data is temporary and expires when you close your browser. Server logs are retained for a limited period for security purposes.

## 6. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal information, there is generally no data to access or delete. If you have questions, contact us at [admin@graveyardjokes.com](mailto:admin@graveyardjokes.com).

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted with an updated effective date.
